# Using your patient portal

Your clinic gives you a sign-in for the portal. You sign in with your email, then enter the **6-digit code** Cliniva emails you. It works on a phone.

## What you can do

| Menu | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your home screen |
| **My Appointments** | Your booked and past appointments |
| **My Prescriptions** | The prescriptions your doctors have given you |
| **My Bills** | Your bills and what is paid. If the clinic accepts online payment you can pay a bill here by card, UPI or net banking |
| **My Profile** | The details the clinic holds about you |
| **Privacy** | Your privacy choices and the clinic's notice |

## Pay a bill

1. Open **My Bills** and choose the bill.
2. Choose how to pay. If online payment is not offered, pay at the clinic desk.
3. After paying, the bill shows as **PAID**.

## Your privacy

Open **Privacy** to see the clinic's privacy notice and your choices. A child's choices are made by a parent or guardian.

## Common problems

- **"I did not get my code."** Wait a minute, check spam, then press **Resend OTP**.
- **"My details are wrong."** Check **My Profile**. If you cannot change something there, ask the front desk to correct it.
