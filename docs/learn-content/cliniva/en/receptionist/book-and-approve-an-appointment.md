# Book and approve an appointment

## Book

1. Open **Appointments** and choose the booking screen (**Book**).
2. **Step 1:** pick the doctor and a day and time. Only times inside the doctor's weekly hours are offered. If nothing is offered, set the doctor's hours first (see [Set up your clinic](../admin/set-up-your-clinic.md)). Press **Next Step**.
3. **Step 2:** find the patient by search and select them. Press **Next Step**.
4. **Step 3:** check the summary under **Confirm Appointment** and press **Confirm**.
5. You land on the clinic's **Appointments** list with a confirmation, and the new booking is in the day's list.

## Approve

A new booking **waits for approval**. On **Appointments** find it in the day's list and press **Approve**. Approving lets the doctor start the consultation.

## On the day

When the patient arrives, the doctor opens the appointment and presses **Start consultation**: see [Run a consultation](../doctor/run-a-consultation.md). Afterwards the bill is made from the prescription: [Bill a visit](../billing/bill-a-visit-and-take-payment.md).

## Common problems

- **"The doctor shows no times."** The doctor has no weekly hours, or the day has none.
- **"I cannot find the patient."** Search by name or phone. Register them first if they are new.
- **"Start consultation is missing."** The appointment has not been approved yet.
