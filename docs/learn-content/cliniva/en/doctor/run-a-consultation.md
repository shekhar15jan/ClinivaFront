# Run a consultation

## Before you start

The front desk must have **approved** the appointment. An appointment that is still waiting for approval has no **Start consultation** link.

## Steps

1. Open **Appointments** and find the patient in the day's list.
2. Press **Start consultation**. The consultation workspace opens with one empty medicine row.
3. Write the **chief complaint** and the **diagnosis**.
4. Record the vitals you took, for example blood pressure (such as 120/80) and temperature.
5. **Add medicines:** type in the medicine row and pick the medicine from the clinic's catalogue by pressing its name. Set the **duration** in days. Add more rows for more medicines.
6. Press **Finish & Print**. Cliniva saves the consultation and the prescription together and tells you **"Consultation and prescription saved"**.
7. You land on the saved prescription, which shows the patient, you as the doctor, and each medicine. Print it from there if the patient needs paper.

Billing is **not** part of your steps: the front desk bills from the prescription ([Bill a visit](../billing/bill-a-visit-and-take-payment.md)).

## With no network (installed app)

If the connection drops while you save, the consultation note is kept **on your device** and the screen says so. It is sent once, automatically, when you are back online. Add the prescription after that, because it needs the saved consultation.

## Common problems

- **"Start consultation is missing."** The appointment is not approved yet. Ask the front desk to approve it.
- **"My medicine is not in the list."** Ask your pharmacist or admin to add it to the clinic's medicine catalogue.
- **"It said saved on this device."** There was no network. Leave the app open or installed: it sends when the connection returns.
