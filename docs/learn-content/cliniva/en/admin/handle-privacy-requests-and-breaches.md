# Handle privacy: notice, patient requests, breaches and retention

The **Privacy** page (needs the *Privacy management* permission) is where the clinic keeps what the privacy laws ask for: who patients can contact, the notice they are shown, requests they make about their data, breach records, and how long records are kept.

## Who patients contact

At the top, fill **Name**, **Email** and **Phone** of the privacy officer (in India the grievance officer; in the EU and UK the data protection officer) and press **Save**. They are shown in the privacy notice and in the patient portal.

Also set **A guardian consents for patients under**: 18 (India DPDP, Gulf), 16 (GDPR default), 15, 14 or 13 (UK GDPR). For patients younger than this, consent is recorded for a parent or guardian.

## Publish the privacy notice

1. Under **Privacy notice**, start from the text provided and make it yours. Have your lawyer check it.
2. Press the publish button. This makes a new version and patients see it from then on. **Published versions** lists the earlier ones; tap a version to read it.

## Requests about data

Patients may ask to see, correct or erase their data. Record each one the day it arrives.

1. Under **Requests about data** press **Record a request**. Choose **What is asked** and **Received by** (*At the desk*, *Email* or *Post*), and fill **From**, **Email or phone** and **Details**. Save it; *Request recorded* appears.
2. Answer within **30 days**: the shortest deadline among the laws Cliniva serves.
3. On the request choose the status: **Open**, **In progress**, **Done** or **Refused**. To finish it (Done or Refused) type **The answer given (needed to close)**, then press **Update**.
4. The filter button hides finished requests so you see only those waiting.

## Data breaches

Record every breach, even a small one. The regulator must be told **within 72 hours of finding it**.

1. Under **Data breaches** press **Record a breach**. Fill **What happened**, **Kind**, **Severity** (Low, Medium, High), **Found on**, **People affected** and **Data involved** (for example *Names, phone numbers, diagnoses*). Save it.
2. Each breach shows when the regulator is due (*due by …*, or *OVERDUE since …* in red). As things happen press the buttons on it: **Contained now**, then type the **Regulator's reference** and press **Regulator told now**, then **People told now**.
3. Press **Close** when it is dealt with.

## How long records are kept

Under **How long records are kept** set the **Years**, counted from the patient's last visit, test, stay or bill, and choose whether it runs automatically. Press **Save** (*Retention saved*). The card says how many patients are past the period.

- To remove names now press **Anonymise them now**, then **Yes, anonymise**. Name, contact, address, ABHA and portal login are removed for good; the medical and billing record stays without a name.
- To keep one patient's record (for example for an insurance claim) open the patient, find **Keeping the record**, type a reason and press **Keep on legal hold**. **Lift legal hold** releases it.
- To anonymise one patient on request (erasure) use **Anonymise this patient** on their page, type why, and confirm with **Yes, anonymise**. *Patient anonymised* appears.

## A patient's own consents

On a patient's page, **Privacy and consent** lists each consent and how it was given (for example **In person** or **On paper**). For a patient under the guardian age, enter the **Parent or guardian** and **Relation** first (*Mother, Father, Guardian*). The download button gives a copy of the patient's data.

## Check your work

- *Request recorded*, *Retention saved* and *Patient anonymised* appear after the matching steps.
- A breach that is not yet reported to the regulator shows its due time; once told it shows the date and your reference.
