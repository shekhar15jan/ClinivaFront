# Read the clinic's reports and analytics

Cliniva has two screens for the numbers: **Reports & Analytics** for the everyday clinic picture, and **Analytics** for the hospital view across lab, radiology, theatre, insurance and the wards. Both only count your own clinic's data.

## Reports & Analytics (clinic overview)

Open **Reports** in the menu. At the top are the tiles:

- **Total Revenue**
- **Appointments Today**
- **Active Doctors** (shown with "Currently available")
- **Outstanding Dues**: money billed but not yet collected

Below them are the charts:

- **Monthly Appointment Trends**
- **Revenue Breakdown (Monthly)**
- **Bills Status**: how many bills are **Paid** and what is still open
- **Doctor Performance**: which doctors see the most patients

If a number fails to load, press **Retry** on that card.

### Take the numbers out

Under **Export Reports**, press **Export as CSV** for a spreadsheet or **Export as PDF** for a printable report.

## Analytics (hospital view)

Open **Analytics** in the menu (the clinic's plan must include it).

1. Choose the period: **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** or **1 year**, or pick your own **From** and **To** dates.
2. The tiles show **Billed** (with the number of bills), **Bed occupancy**, **Admissions · discharges** and **Surgeries done**.
3. **Revenue by source** shows how much came from each part of the clinic.
4. **Day by day** shows the trend across the period.
5. Further down are sections for **Lab**, **Radiology**, **Operation theatre**, **Insurance** and **Admissions by type**.

## Common problems

- **"Reports or Analytics is missing from the menu."** Your role needs the permission, and the clinic's plan must include the **Reports** or **Analytics** module.
- **"A figure looks too low."** Check the period at the top; the default is a recent window, not the clinic's whole history.
